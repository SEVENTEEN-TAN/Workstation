First stable release of Personal Workstation.

- Public career profile with structured projects, experience, skills, activities, resume, and public OKR views.

- Read-only Obsidian knowledge vault indexing, private publication drafts, collections, and immutable public article attachments.
- SQLite migrations, authentication, deployment guidance, and verification coverage for core workflows.
